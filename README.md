### 👋 About me

Hey, I'm **Pankaj Kumar** — an AI Engineer based in Gurugram, Haryana. I build reliable LLM systems, machine-learning infrastructure and distributed backends that hold up in production. Right now I'm working on **Physical AI** — edge AI engineering and robotics.

- 🤖 **Physical AI & robotics:** edge AI runtimes, on-device inference and robot intelligence
- 🧠 **ML infrastructure:** training-data pipelines, LLM fine-tuning workflows, multi-architecture container images, AWS ECR and model serving
- ⚡ **Low-latency trading systems:** led engineering for algorithmic trading across 15+ concurrent accounts with Python and Golang services
- 📞 **Telephony & messaging at scale:** 100,000+ daily-call IVR systems, WhatsApp integrations, Kafka pipelines and infrastructure across 20+ servers
- 🛠️ **Independent work:** CyberSentinel, TokenFin and Missy OS — products at the intersection of AI, security, FinOps and developer infrastructure
